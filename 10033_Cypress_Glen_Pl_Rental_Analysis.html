What the data tells us

Pattern Recognition
No. 1
The market is rewarding accurate pricing, not aspiration.
Seven of seven leased comps closed at or within 2.3% of list. Median DOM landed at 27 days, but the two longest cycles (92 and 85 days) both belonged to properties that started above market and required time to find their level. Properly priced 3/2s in this submarket are leasing in under 30 days.
No. 2
The lone active is a directional signal worth respecting.
861 Blackgum Ct is asking $2,500 with only 7 days on market — too early to call as either a benchmark or an outlier. What it does tell us is that at least one owner believes the market will bear above the leased range, and we won't know until that listing is tested. The strategy here is to position the subject confidently within the upper band and let first-week showing activity tell us where to settle.
No. 3
Year built matters less than condition and finish.
The leased range spans 1990 to 2026 construction with remarkably tight rent spread ($2,059 – $2,300). At 1997 vintage, the subject's age is not a constraint. The premium rents in this set were tied to rent-ready condition — exactly the expectation you've set for this property.
No. 4
Square footage and condition support a confident upper-band entry.
At 1,251 sqft the subject sits between 10002 Massey ($2,300) and 11414 Pinewood Cove ($2,295), and slightly under 11301 Cypress Leaf which closed over ask at $2,250. With show-ready condition as the baseline, a $2,350 list price represents a measured stretch — anchored to comps but positioned to capture upward movement if the market is shifting.

My Recommendation

Launch at $2,350. Monitor week one.

This is a measured stretch above the leased range, supported by show-ready condition and the active listing testing the upper band. The strategy is intentional: start high, let the market respond, and read the data quickly.

Strong first-week showing activity with applications inside 7–10 days confirms the price. If we see traffic but no applications by day 7, the natural adjustment is a $50 reduction to $2,300. If activity is light by day 10, drop to the $2,250 floor — still aligned with the leased median and competitively positioned. This approach captures upside without committing to vacancy carrying cost.
